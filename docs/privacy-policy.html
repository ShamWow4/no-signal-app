Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the No Signal! mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to provide information about Audio/Visual gigs, training, and events in the New Orleans area. We prioritize your privacy and collect minimal data.

A. Device Information

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain information about your device, including information about your operating system, device type, and app version. This helps us optimize the App for your device.

B. Push Notifications (Optional)

If you choose to enable Push Notifications, we generate and store a Push Token associated with your device to send you relevant alerts (such as new gigs or convention updates). You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information primarily to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality.
  • Send you push notifications (if enabled).
  • Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve your experience.

3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. The App relies on Firebase (by Google) for database hosting (Firestore) and Cloud Messaging (for push notifications). Firebase may collect standard diagnostic and usage data.
